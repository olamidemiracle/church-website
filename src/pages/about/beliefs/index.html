Statement of Faith

What we believe, in plain terms.

About / Statement of Faith
The Scriptures
We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word — fully true and our final authority for faith and life.
God
We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
Jesus Christ
We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, who died for our sins and rose bodily from the dead.
Salvation
We believe salvation is a gift of God's grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
The Holy Spirit
We believe the Holy Spirit indwells every believer, empowering a life that reflects Christ.
The Church
We believe the Church is God's family on earth, called to worship, disciple, and serve together.
Eternity
We believe in the bodily return of Christ and the eternal life promised to all who believe in Him.
