Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you share with us through this website — including prayer requests, membership applications, contact messages, and event RSVPs.

Information We Collect

We collect the information you voluntarily provide through our forms, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message or request you submit. We do not require you to create an account to browse this site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to prayer requests, inquiries, and messages you send us.
  • To process membership applications and visitor follow-up.
  • To keep you informed about church events and announcements, where you've asked to hear from us.
  • To improve our website and ministry programs.

Confidential Prayer Requests

If you mark a prayer request as confidential, it is only made visible to designated church staff and is not shared publicly or with the wider congregation.

How We Protect Your Information

Submitted information is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized church staff and administrators. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate this website, including hosting, database, email notification, and payment processing providers. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their specific function.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use our website, helping us improve content and navigation. This data is used in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to request that we update, correct, or delete personal information you've submitted through this website.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the involvement of a parent or guardian.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us.
